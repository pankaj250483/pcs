Curriculum Breakdown

Comprehensive Syllabus

A structured learning path taking you from basic formulas to advanced automation.

Part 1: Core Fundamentals & Intermediate Features

10 Hours

Master the grid, data formatting, basic formulas, and visualization rules to build clean spreadsheets.

  • Ribbon, Quick Access Toolbar, and Workbook Management
  • Custom Number Formatting (Currency, Text, Hidden masks)
  • Flash Fill, AutoFill tricks, and productivity shortcuts
  • Relative, Absolute ($A$1), and Mixed ($A1) Cell Referencing

  • Basic statistical formulas: SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, COUNTA, MAX, MIN
  • Logical testing: IF, AND, OR logic trees
  • Data Cleanup: TRIM, TEXTSPLIT, CONCAT, UPPER, LOWER
  • Date Functions: TODAY, DATEDIF, EOMONTH, WORKDAY

  • Converting ranges to Structured Excel Tables (Ctrl + T)
  • Conditional Formatting Rules, Data Bars, and Icon Sets
  • Sorting and Advanced Multi-Level Filters

Part 2: Advanced Data Analysis & Formulas

12 Hours

Leverage dynamic arrays, sophisticated lookups, and pivot charts for complex analytical tasks.

  • Mastering XLOOKUP & XMATCH over legacy VLOOKUP
  • Two-way Lookups with INDEX + MATCH
  • Dynamic Arrays: FILTER, UNIQUE, SORT, SORTBY, SEQUENCE

  • Conditional Summaries: SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, AVERAGEIFS
  • Building Pivot Tables, Field Settings & % of Grand Total
  • Interactive Dashboard Slicers & Timelines
  • What-If Analysis: Goal Seek, Data Tables, & Scenario Manager

Part 3: Power Query, Power Pivot & DAX

10 Hours

Automate data extraction, transformation, loading (ETL), and construct scalable relational data models.

  • Importing data from CSVs, Folders, Web, & SQL Databases
  • Unpivoting columns, Appending, and Merging datasets (Joins)
  • Creating automated repeatable data cleaning workflows

  • Creating Star Schemas & Relationships in Power Pivot
  • DAX Fundamentals: Explicit Measures vs. Calculated Columns
  • Essential DAX: CALCULATE, RELATED, SUMX, ALL

Part 4: VBA & Macro Automation

8 Hours

Eliminate routine manual work by building customized scripts and interactive user tools.

  • Recording, Editing, and Debugging recorded Macros
  • VBA Syntax: Modules, Subroutines, Variables, and Logic Loops (For Each, If...Then)
  • Creating Action Buttons & Custom Form Controls
  • Automating Multi-Workbook consolidation scripts
